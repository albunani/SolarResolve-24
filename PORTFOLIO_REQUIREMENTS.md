Use a modern dark theme with a professional AI, developer, and engineering aesthetic.

Recommended characteristics:

- deep charcoal or near-black background;
- slightly lighter elevated project-card surfaces;
- high-contrast off-white primary text;
- muted cool-grey secondary text;
- one restrained technical or energy-oriented accent color;
- subtle borders and controlled shadows;
- generous whitespace;
- crisp technical hierarchy; and
- minimal decorative noise.

- gaming-style interfaces;
- generic circuit-board backgrounds;
- distracting particles;

- Ensure content remains available if the web font fails.

## 15. Interaction and motion

Implement smooth, restrained feedback for:

- project cards;
- technology badges;
- primary buttons;
- project links; and
- social links.

### Interaction requirements

- Project cards may lift slightly or strengthen their border on hover.
- Badges may adjust background, border, or position subtly.
- Buttons and links must have clear default, hover, pressed, and keyboard-focus states.
- Keyboard focus must be at least as clear as hover.
- Motion should normally complete within approximately 150–250 ms.
- Use transform and opacity where practical to avoid jank.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing or minimizing nonessential motion.

Avoid large transforms, bouncing, continuous animation, slow reveals, or motion that delays access to content.

## 17. Accessibility requirements

- Use semantic landmarks and a logical heading structure.
- Use one clear `h1` containing the portfolio owner's name or primary page identity.
- Ensure interactive elements are keyboard reachable and operable.
- Provide visible `:focus-visible` styles.
- Meet WCAG AA contrast for text and essential controls.
- Do not use color alone to communicate link state, category, or availability.
- Give live links descriptive accessible names.
- Hide decorative icons from assistive technology.
- Give a real profile photograph meaningful alternative text.
- Use touch targets of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels where practical.
- Preserve content and functionality at 200% browser zoom.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## 18. Performance requirements

- Optimize any profile photograph before deployment.

- Avoid large video or decorative background assets.
- Prevent layout shift by reserving avatar dimensions.
- Load only the required font resources.
- Keep hover effects CSS-based where practical.
- Ensure the core content remains readable if external font delivery fails.
