# SolarPeer 360 Documentation

This directory contains a product, design, content, operations, safety, privacy, SEO, analytics, and QA documentation baseline for the public SolarPeer 360 website at <https://solarpeer360.vercel.app/>.

The baseline was prepared from the public website reviewed on 22 September 2026. It describes what the site currently communicates and separates observed facts from recommendations or items that require internal confirmation. It is not a substitute for signed engineering, electrical-safety, legal, privacy, or regulatory documentation.

## Documentation rules

1. Mark unverified internal details as `TBD` instead of guessing.
2. Date claims that can become stale, including pilot locations, prices, deployment counts, and app availability.
3. Keep customer-facing claims traceable to evidence.
4. Record material product or design choices in `DECISIONS.md`.
5. Update affected documents in the same change as the product or website.

## Current public baseline

- Pilot interest is being collected in Lagos, Niger State, and FCT Abuja.
- The public price assumption is ₦250 per kWh and is not presented as final.
- Buyers may pay in cash or by transfer according to the proposed pilot design.
- The Android app is not publicly downloadable.
- The site states that five field deployments were completed in Minna, Niger State.
- The public waitlist supports buyers, solar owners, installers, and cluster partners.

Review these statements before any release because they are time-sensitive.
