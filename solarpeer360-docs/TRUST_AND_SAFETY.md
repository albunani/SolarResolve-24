# Trust and Safety

## Scope

SolarPeer connects energy infrastructure, people, payments, and personal data. Trust and safety therefore covers electrical work, equipment, participant verification, claims, money handling, privacy, incident response, and public communication.

This document defines product requirements only. Qualified Nigerian electrical, legal, privacy, insurance, and regulatory professionals must approve the operating policy before deployment.

## Safety principles

1. No connection before site assessment and approval.
2. A solar owner's essential reserve takes priority over shared energy.
3. Buyer power limits must reflect assessed appliances and cable capacity.
4. Only approved installers may install, commission, alter, or remove equipment.
5. Meters, receipts, and operating records must be understandable to both sides.
6. A participant must be able to report a hazard without using the app.
7. Commercial pressure must never override a safety stop.

## Verification

### Solar owners

Verify identity, authority over the premises and system, equipment condition, battery and inverter capacity, existing loads, available reserve, cable route, and consent to ongoing monitoring or inspection.

### Buyers

Verify identity and location, intended appliances, required power limit, safe wiring, meter position, and understanding of supply limitations.

### Installers

Verify identity, qualifications, insurance where applicable, references, service area, equipment competence, incident history, safety procedures, and signed operating obligations. Required credentials are `TBD` pending professional review.

### Cluster partners

Verify authority to organise the named community and prohibit claims that imply guaranteed selection, employment, partnership, investment, or free installation.

## Installation controls

- Document assessment, design, approvals, materials, protective devices, cable route, commissioning tests, photographs, and sign-off.
- Give each participant an emergency isolation and contact procedure.
- Tag equipment with an identifier and support contact.
- Record any later alteration and require re-assessment when loads change materially.

## Commercial trust

- Publish current price status and effective date.
- Issue receipts to buyer and solar owner for every sale.
- State commission, fees, refunds, failed-payment treatment, and dispute process in signed terms.
- Do not accept public-web payments until the transaction, reconciliation, refund, and fraud controls are approved.

## Incident severity

| Level | Example | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Shock, fire, smoke, exposed live conductor | Tell people to keep clear, contact emergency services where needed, isolate only if safe, alert safety lead immediately |
| High | Overheating, repeated protective trip, damaged cable | Suspend affected supply and dispatch an approved installer |
| Medium | Meter mismatch, unexplained credit loss | Preserve records, investigate, and pause disputed settlement if policy allows |
| Low | App, receipt, or information issue | Normal support workflow |

Response owners, phone trees, service targets, and report templates are `TBD`.

## Public trust requirements

Before wider launch, publish named leadership, company registration details, provider-verification steps, installer standards, fault reporting, equipment ownership, warranties, data practices, and complaint escalation.
