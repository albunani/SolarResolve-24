## Maintenance

For every release:

1. Move completed items from `Unreleased` into a dated version.
2. Link the changed requirement, decision, or operating policy.
3. Identify public claims that need a new evidence or review date.
4. Record privacy, safety, and pricing changes explicitly.
5. Keep historical entries; correct mistakes with a new note rather than rewriting history silently.
