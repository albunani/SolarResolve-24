# Pilot Operations

## Objective

Turn public interest into safe, geographically viable, and commercially understandable pilot clusters without promising service before assessment and signed terms.

## Operating stages

```text
Waitlist
  -> Data quality review
  -> Community clustering
  -> Buyer and solar-owner qualification
  -> Technical assessment
  -> Commercial and consent confirmation
  -> Installation and commissioning
  -> Controlled activation
  -> Monitoring, support, and review
```

## Intake

- Review new entries for completeness, duplicates, consent, location, and role.
- Keep unsupported-state entries on an expansion list with separate expectations.
- Do not contact a person through a channel outside the consent and privacy policy.
- Record source, date, reference, owner, and next action.

## Community clustering

A viable cluster requires enough compatible demand, a technically suitable nearby solar owner, safe cable routes, installer availability, and an approved operating model. Public sign-up volume may influence priority but must not be the only criterion.

Publish the selection method before inviting communities to compete. Thresholds, distance rules, review cadence, and tie-breaking are `TBD`.

## Qualification

### Buyer

Confirm premises, intended appliances, operating schedule, ability to understand prepaid energy and power limits, preferred payment method, and willingness to sign pilot terms.

### Solar owner

Confirm ownership or authority, system condition, reserve requirements, available capacity, expected generation and load patterns, equipment compatibility, and willingness to accept settlement terms.

### Installer

Follow the verification requirements in [Trust and safety](TRUST_AND_SAFETY.md). Maintain assignment, assessment, installation, commissioning, and maintenance records.

## Assessment and approval

Each proposed connection needs a documented assessment, design decision, capacity allocation, power limit, equipment list, cable route, protection plan, participant sign-off, and approval by the designated technical owner.

## Commercial readiness

Before activation, provide signed terms covering price, commission, meter or hub terms, deposits if any, receipts, refunds, supply limitations, maintenance, suspension, complaints, and exit. Public assumptions must be reconciled with the final terms.

## Activation checklist

- Approved assessment and installation record
- Commissioning tests passed
- Meter identifiers linked to correct participants
- Initial balance and power limit confirmed
- Receipt channels tested
- Emergency isolation explained
- Support contacts provided
- Privacy and monitoring notices acknowledged
- Pilot agreement signed

## Support and incidents

Use one case reference across call, WhatsApp, email, app, and installer work. Electrical hazards follow the incident process immediately. Commercial or meter disputes preserve transaction and device records before investigation.

## Pilot metrics

- Qualified clusters and participants
- Assessment pass rate
- Installation and activation cycle time
- Delivered kWh and availability
- Meter or settlement discrepancies
- Safety events and near misses
- Support volume and resolution time
- Participant retention and satisfaction

Metric definitions, owners, and review cadence are `TBD`.
