# Product Requirements

## Purpose

SolarPeer 360 helps people and small businesses buy measured, prepaid solar energy from a nearby solar owner. The public website must explain the model, establish trust, qualify pilot participants, and collect usable waitlist interest without implying that service is already generally available.

## Product goals

1. Explain local solar sharing in language a shop owner, student, household, or solar owner can understand.
2. Convert qualified visitors into pilot waitlist entries or direct conversations.
3. Collect enough location and role information to identify viable community clusters.
4. Set accurate expectations about pricing, availability, installation, safety, and the pilot stage.
5. Give installers and community organisers a clear path to express interest.

## Non-goals

- Selling electricity or collecting payment through the public website.
- Promising continuous power, fixed savings, or a deployment date.
- Publishing the Android application before participants are approved and their meters are ready.
- Treating a waitlist submission as a supply agreement, installation booking, or account creation.

## Primary users

| User | Main need | Primary action |
|---|---|---|
| Shop or small business | Keep lights, refrigeration, and small equipment running | Join as a buyer |
| Student residence | Coordinate reliable light and charging for a residence | Join as a buyer or cluster |
| Household | Run fans, lights, and small appliances | Join as a buyer |
| Solar owner | Earn from measured spare energy without risking household supply | Join as a solar owner |
| Installer | Assess sites, fit meters, and support clusters | Join as an installer |
| Cluster partner | Organise a street, estate, campus, or community | Join as a cluster partner |

## Core requirements

### Public explanation

- State that energy travels by cable and usage is measured in kWh.
- Distinguish energy purchased in kWh from the buyer's power limit in watts.
- Explain that a site assessment occurs before any connection.
- State that a solar owner's own supply reserve comes first.
- Explain payment, receipt, metering, and commission at a high level.

### Waitlist

- Ask for the participant role first.
- Show buyer-only questions only when the buyer role is selected.
- Collect name, Nigerian mobile number, state, community or area, optional notes, and explicit contact consent.
- Offer optional location capture with a clear purpose statement.
- Confirm that no payment, deposit, or app account is required.
- Return a reference after successful submission.

### Trust

- Date all pilot evidence and assumptions.
- Distinguish field activity from customer outcomes.
- Identify the company, responsible team, provider-verification process, installer standards, and support route before public launch.

## Success measures

- Waitlist completion rate by acquisition source and user role.
- Percentage of valid, contactable submissions.
- Number of sign-ups per usable community cluster.
- Time from submission to first contact.
- Percentage of qualified solar owners and buyer sites after assessment.
- Support-contact rate caused by unclear pricing, eligibility, or process.

## Constraints and open questions

- Final pilot price, meter terms, hub terms, commissions, deposits, and installation charges are not confirmed publicly.
- Community-selection thresholds and review dates are not published.
- Provider-verification, fault response, equipment ownership, warranties, and dispute handling require internal confirmation.
- Regulatory and electrical-safety requirements must be reviewed by qualified Nigerian professionals before deployment.
