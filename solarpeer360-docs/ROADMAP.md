# Roadmap

## Prioritisation model

Priorities reflect user risk, trust, conversion impact, and dependency order. Dates require team capacity and are therefore not assigned here.

## Now: pilot-readiness foundation

1. Publish named company and team information that can be verified.
2. Document provider verification, installer standards, equipment ownership, maintenance, fault response, and complaints.
3. Finalise privacy, consent, retention, vendor, and data-access controls.
4. Define pilot community-selection criteria and contact timelines.
5. Confirm price, commission, meter, hub, deposit, refund, and receipt terms.
6. Add WhatsApp as an above-the-fold secondary CTA.
7. Reduce homepage length by 25–35% without removing material conditions.
8. Change the pricing presets to a radio group and add Pricing to primary navigation.
9. Add canonical URLs, social-sharing image metadata, sitemap, and verified structured data.
10. Implement and test the full waitlist error and success states.

## Next: controlled pilot operations

1. Build internal lead review and community-clustering workflow.
2. Create separate qualification checklists for buyers, solar owners, installers, and cluster partners.
3. Approve assessment, installation, commissioning, activation, and incident templates.
4. Establish receipt, balance, dispute, and reconciliation procedures.
5. Add appliance examples for each power limit using reviewed assumptions.
6. Publish a dedicated safety and verification page.
7. Implement privacy-safe funnel analytics and operational qualification metrics.
8. Establish support service targets and cross-channel case references.

## Later: validated expansion

1. Publish dated pilot outcomes with methodology and limitations.
2. Introduce participant app access only after meter readiness and secure onboarding.
3. Add self-service balance, usage, receipt, and support features based on pilot evidence.
4. Expand locations using documented technical, demand, and operational criteria.
5. Build installer capacity and quality monitoring.
6. Add role-specific public pages only when evidence supports distinct content.

## Do not build yet

- Public app download before participant onboarding is ready
- Online payment before reconciliation, refund, fraud, and dispute controls
- Nationwide availability claims
- Public savings calculator without defensible appliance, tariff, and generator assumptions
- Social proof that cannot be verified or used with consent

## Exit criteria for pilot readiness

- Safety and operating policies approved by responsible professionals
- Privacy notice and data map match the implementation
- Waitlist submissions are reliable, consented, deduplicated, and recoverable
- Community and participant qualification process is staffed
- Installation, commissioning, support, and incident procedures are rehearsed
- Public claims are evidence-backed and dated
- QA release blockers in [QA test plan](QA_TEST_PLAN.md) are clear
