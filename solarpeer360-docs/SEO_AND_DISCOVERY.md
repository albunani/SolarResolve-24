# SEO and Discovery

## Objectives

Help Nigerian users, community organisers, installers, partners, and journalists find accurate information about the SolarPeer pilot. Search presentation must reflect pilot status and must not imply nationwide service availability.

## Current baseline

The reviewed site includes unique titles, meta descriptions, Open Graph titles and descriptions, a Twitter summary card, and `og:locale=en_NG`. The homepage did not expose a canonical URL, Open Graph image, Twitter image, or JSON-LD structured data during the review.

## Page targets

| Page | Search intent | Suggested title direction |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Shared/prepaid solar energy in Nigeria | Prepaid solar energy from a nearby provider · SolarPeer 360 |
| Pilot | Join a local solar pilot | Join the SolarPeer pilot waitlist |
| About | Company and operating stage | About SolarPeer 360 |
| Help | Eligibility, price, supply, and contact | SolarPeer help and pilot questions |
| App | Official app availability | Official SolarPeer Android app status |
| Privacy | Data use and participant rights | SolarPeer privacy notice |

Keep titles below roughly 60 characters where practical and descriptions around 140–160 characters without truncating material qualifications.

## Technical requirements

- Add a self-referencing canonical URL to every indexable page.
- Publish `robots.txt` and an XML sitemap.
- Add one branded social image of at least 1200 by 630 pixels.
- Add `og:image`, image dimensions, and equivalent Twitter metadata.
- Return correct 404 and redirect status codes.
- Avoid indexing query-parameter variants of the waitlist.
- Use stable, lowercase URLs and permanent redirects for moved content.
- Preserve meaningful server-rendered headings and links.

## Structured data

Use schema.org only for verifiable public information:

- `Organization` with official name, URL, logo, contact point, and verified address when publishable.
- `WebSite` for site identity.
- `FAQPage` only when questions and answers are visible on the same indexed page and current search-engine policy permits it.
- Do not use `Product`, review ratings, service area, or offers until the public facts and eligibility support them.

## Content opportunities

- What kWh and watts mean for shops and homes
- How a solar-site assessment works
- What appliances fit 500 W, 1,000 W, and 1,500 W limits
- How solar owners protect their own reserve
- Pilot updates by dated community or field activity

Every article should lead to the appropriate waitlist role and display the pilot status.

## Local discovery

Use consistent naming for Lagos, Niger State, and FCT Abuja. Do not create city landing pages until each page contains genuinely distinct eligibility, evidence, and contact information.

## Measurement

Track indexed pages, branded and non-branded queries, click-through rate, organic waitlist conversions, broken links, duplicate metadata, and changes in misleading-query traffic.
